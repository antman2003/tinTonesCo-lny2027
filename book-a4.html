十二生肖大赛跑
Shí'èr Shēngxiào Dà Sàipǎo
The Great Zodiac Race
1. 很久很久以前，玉皇大帝决定用动物的名字来命名年份。
Hěnjiǔ hěnjiǔ yǐqián, Yùhuáng Dàdì juédìng yòng dòngwù de míngzi lái mìngmíng niánfèn.
Long, long ago, the Jade Emperor decided to name the years after animals.
2. 他邀请所有的动物参加一场渡河大赛。
Tā yāoqǐng suǒyǒu de dòngwù cānjiā yì chǎng dùhé dàsài.
He invited all the animals to a great race across the river.
3. 前十二名到达终点的动物，每一个都会有一个年份用它的名字命名。
Qián shí'èr míng dàodá zhōngdiǎn de dòngwù, měi yí gè dōu huì yǒu yí gè niánfèn yòng tā de míngzi mìngmíng.
The first twelve animals to reach the finish line would each have a year named after them.
4
4. 聪明的老鼠骑在牛的背上，在最后一刻跳到了前面。
Cōngming de lǎoshǔ qí zài niú de bèi shang, zài zuìhòu yíkè tiào dào le qiánmiàn.
The clever rat rode on the ox's back and jumped ahead at the very last moment.
5. 老鼠得了第一，牛得了第二。
Lǎoshǔ dé le dì yī, niú dé le dì èr.
The rat came first, and the ox came second.
6. 老虎用力地游啊游，得了第三。
Lǎohǔ yònglì de yóu a yóu, dé le dì sān.
The tiger swam with all his might and came third.
7. 兔子踩着石头和木头，跳过了河，得了第四。
Tùzi cǎi zhe shítou hé mùtou, tiào guò le hé, dé le dì sì.
The rabbit hopped across on stones and logs and came fourth.
8. 龙飞过了河，得了第五。
Lóng fēi guò le hé, dé le dì wǔ.
The dragon flew across the river and came fifth.
9. 蛇游过了河，得了第六。
Shé yóu guò le hé, dé le dì liù.
The snake swam across the river and came sixth.
5
10. 马跑过了河，得了第七。
Mǎ pǎo guò le hé, dé le dì qī.
The horse ran across the river and came seventh.
11. 善良的羊、猴子和公鸡一起乘着木筏过河，互相帮助。
Shànliáng de yáng, hóuzi hé gōngjī yìqǐ chéng zhe mùfá guò hé, hùxiāng bāngzhù.
The kind goat, the monkey, and the rooster crossed the river together on a raft, helping each other.
12. 羊得了第八，猴子得了第九，公鸡得了第十。
Yáng dé le dì bā, hóuzi dé le dì jiǔ, gōngjī dé le dì shí.
The goat came eighth, the monkey ninth, and the rooster tenth.
13. 狗游过了河，得了第十一。
Gǒu yóu guò le hé, dé le dì shí yī.
The dog swam across the river and came eleventh.
14. 猪跑过了河，得了第十二。
Zhū pǎo guò le hé, dé le dì shí èr.
The pig ran across the river and came twelfth.
15. 这就是十二生肖的故事。
Zhè jiù shì shí'èr shēngxiào de gùshì.
And that is the story of the twelve zodiac animals.

18
做灯笼
Zuò dēnglóng
Make a Paper Lantern
第一步：把纸对折。
Dì yī bù: Bǎ zhǐ duìzhé.
Step 1: Fold the paper in half.
第二步：从对折边开始剪成一条一条的，剪到⏹标记处就停下，不要剪断。
Dì èr bù: Cóng duìzhé biān kāishǐ jiǎn chéng yì tiáo yì tiáo de, jiǎn dào ⏹ biāojì chù jiù tíng xià, bú yào jiǎn duàn.
Step 2: Cut strips from the folded edge, stopping at the ⏹ marks. Do not cut all the way through.
第三步：把纸打开。
Dì sān bù: Bǎ zhǐ dǎkāi.
Step 3: Open the paper up.
第四步：把纸卷成灯笼的形状，用胶带粘好。
Dì sì bù: Bǎ zhǐ juǎn chéng dēnglóng de xíngzhuàng, yòng jiāodài zhān hǎo.
Step 4: Roll the paper into a lantern shape and tape it closed.
第五步：做一个提手，粘在灯笼上面。
Dì wǔ bù: Zuò yí gè tíshǒu, zhān zài dēnglóng shàngmiàn.
Step 5: Make a handle and attach it to the top of the lantern.
第六步：装饰你的灯笼，就完成了！
Dì liù bù: Zhuāngshì nǐ de dēnglóng, jiù wánchéng le!
Step 6: Decorate your lantern — you are done!
